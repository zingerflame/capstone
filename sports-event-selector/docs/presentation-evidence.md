## Slide 2 — How the model works

Implementation: src/sports_events/pretrained.py and core.py. Frozen ImageNet ResNet-18, full-frame letterbox224, 512 scene features; scoreboard grayscale16x8 plus presence and delta-time features. Feature differences between adjacent normalized scene vectors strictly inside the current window. Two Transformer layers, width64, four heads, learned ten-position embeddings. Max six positives per ten-frame window; stride one; nonmandatory frames need support from >=3 real windows. Votes ranked by agreement/confidence then validation-calibrated budget; final sequences contain two or three frames. First two and last two frames always retained as context. Expanded primary has budget factor3. Scoreboard is pixels, not OCR. Sources: https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.resnet18.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10213 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05392 . Inspired experiments, not reproductions.

## Slide 4 — Three model iterations

Iteration1 scratch scene/scoreboard CNN plus two-layer Transformer with original v1loss. Iteration2 v2loss balances soft-positive neighborhoods, corrects duplicate capacity, moderates count penalty and warms it up over first six epochs. Iteration3 frozen ResNet18 scene features, scoreboard grayscale, motion difference option, original vs expanded budget. Six training runs: appearance/motion x seeds17/29/43,20epochs each. ## Slide 5 — Training pipeline

Download official broadcasts one at a time. Dataset uses short manually selected intervals, not full-corpus labels. sample_video grayscale64x36 mean delta threshold0.035, nominal10–20fps;25fps quantization can yield8.33fps. Preserve original frame timestamps and scoreboard ROI. ChatGPT visually labels 2–3 retained-frame indices plus descriptions/confidence and explicit negatives; no teacher API call and no independently verified labels. Train/val/test groups split by match6/2/2. Total reviewed184s; training72s with691frames and75anchors, validation24s224frames45anchors, newesttest28s264frames18anchors. FrozenResNet18 caches512D scene features; grayscaleboard128D+presence+dt gives642D. Train temporal selector per clip using 10frame stride1 windows and v2loss: triangular targets1,.75,.5,.25 within±3 filtered frames, weighted BCE, coverage, nearest-GT duplicate constraint and excess decoded-count straight-through surrogate; countweight warms to.2, not an exact derivative through discrete grouping. AdamW3e-4 weightdecay1e-3, clipgrad1,20epochs. Every epoch validates thresholds.1–.7, consensus.5/.65/.8,budgetfactor1/2/3. Three seeds17/29/43 per appearance/motion arm. Primary selected using mean validationF1 then frozen before tests. Final output contains event frame sequences without event-text labels. Source code: broadcasts.py, sampling.py, pilot.py, pretrained.py, experiment3.py, learning.py, core.py.
